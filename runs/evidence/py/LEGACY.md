# Legacy results (pre-PREREG)

All results in runs/*.json and runs/*.jsonl produced before PREREG.md are
pre-registration-era artifacts. They are NOT evidence under the current
protocol. Thresholds, producers, scorers, and families were not frozen before
those runs. They are retained for historical reference only.

Legacy files include (non-exhaustive): runs/fact_recall_proof.json,
runs/sleep_consolidation.json, runs/obscure_agent.json, runs/codebase_agent.json,
and all runs/plastic_core_*.json, runs/sleep_*.json, runs/agent_bench/, etc.
